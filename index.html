Call for Papers

We invite researchers and practitioners to submit original work on cutting-edge robotics technologies for space exploration. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Mechanical Design of Robots
  • Autonomous Mobile Platform
  • Perception and Motion Planning
  • Task-specific Autonomy
  • Robot Simulation Environments for Space Exploration
Note: Special Session papers follow the same submission, review, acceptance, and publication process as regular conference papers.
